### 2. SUMMARY OF KEY SYSTEM ENHANCEMENTS & ARCHITECTURE

The upgraded application incorporates several key operational improvements designed to streamline live event execution:

1. **Modern Single-Screen Touch Control Interface:**
   * Designed specifically for high-speed touch screen workstations, consolidating scoring controls (`+3`, `+2`, `+1`, `-1`), fouls, timeouts, and shot clock functions (`24s` / `14s` with illuminated `SHOT: ON` indicator) onto a single, non-scrolling operator layout.

2. **Intelligent Multi-Line Team Name Engine (Zero Truncation):**
   * Incorporates a pixel-perfect typography measuring engine that automatically wraps extended team names (e.g., *"HOME A LONG THE REALITY"* or *"BARANGAY GINEBRA KINGS"*) across balanced multi-line configurations (up to 2 lines) in bold typography without character truncation or text clipping.

3. **Centered Digital Chronometer & Set Display Alignment:**
   * Precision-engineered horizontal centering for the primary Game Clock (`09:48`) and Set Indicator (`SET 1`), eliminating visual kerning bias and ensuring hardware-grade 7-segment digital aesthetic standards.

4. **Proportioned Commercial Media & Ad Overlay Integration:**
   * Synchronized timeout execution that automatically invokes a split-screen presentation featuring a proportioned **200px Cyan Countdown Ring**, real-time `TIME REMAINING` indicators, and full high-definition commercial video or static banner playback.

5. **Corporate Fitness Exclusive Branding Header:**
   * High-contrast corporate logo integration anchored across the top header of all operator control panels, maintaining corporate identity standards.

### 3. STAFF TRAINING & HANDOVER PROTOCOL

All designated event operators must complete orientation on the following operational protocols prior to live event execution:

* **Protocol Alpha (System Initialization):** Launch the primary control window on the workstation. Drag the presentation display to the secondary arena LED TV monitor and activate fullscreen presentation (`F11`).
* **Protocol Bravo (Chronometer Controls):** Master start/pause operations must be executed via the `SPACEBAR` hotkey or the primary touch button for zero-latency timer toggling.
* **Protocol Charlie (Volleyball Serving Vectors):** Operators must actively toggle the serving vector arrows (`SERVE AWAY ◀` / `SERVE HOME ▶`) upon every side-out or service change.
* **Protocol Delta (Timeout & Commercial Media Execution):** Initiating a timeout immediately activates the commercial ad overlay. The overlay automatically terminates upon timeout expiration or manual cancellation by the operator.
